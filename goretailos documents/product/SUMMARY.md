# Product (single source)

`GORETAILOS_PRODUCT_FEATURES.md` stays because the website shows it at `/docs/features`; it is the
detailed per-module reference. Other product documents were removed (git history).

## What GoRetailOS is
A multi-tenant retail and restaurant business system for small and mid-size shops: offline-capable
POS, online store, inventory, purchasing and procurement, accounting, HR and payroll, CRM and
loyalty, marketing, reports/KPIs and an AI assistant. Multi-currency, multilingual (incl. RTL
storefront).

## Modules
The list of 14 modules is kept only in `src/lib/module-keys.ts` (explained in `docs/guides/GORETAILOS_AGENT_GUIDE.md` §0).

## Business model
3 months free, then per-module plans (licences per shop).

## Positioning
For retailers and restaurants wanting one system instead of separate POS, shop, stock and books.
Not a generic marketplace or a custom-build agency.
